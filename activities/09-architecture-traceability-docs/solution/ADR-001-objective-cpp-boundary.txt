ADR-001: Objective-C++ is the sole language-translation boundary
Status: Accepted
Context: Swift cannot consume arbitrary C++ templates and ownership must be explicit.
Decision: public Objective-C header, C++ includes and RAII ownership only in .mm, immutable DTO returns.
Consequences: extra adapter code; narrow stable contract; independently testable core.
Requirements: REQ-001, REQ-002. Evidence: BridgeContractTests.
